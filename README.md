# Fixopolis

Fixopolis is a civic issue reporting app prototype for **SDG 11: Sustainable Cities and Communities**. It was made for the INFO3315 Human–Computer Interaction group project at the University of Sydney (2026).

Residents report problems such as potholes, dumped rubbish or broken streetlights. They can then follow each report's progress and bump a neighbour's report to show it affects them too.

## Try it

- **Interactive prototype:** https://olibolib.github.io/fixopolis/ (best in Chrome or Edge on a laptop)
- **Clickable PDF prototype (demonstration):** [`pdf/fixopolis-blueprint-demonstration.pdf`](pdf/fixopolis-blueprint-demonstration.pdf)
- **Clickable PDF prototype (user testing):** [`pdf/fixopolis-blueprint-user-testing.pdf`](pdf/fixopolis-blueprint-user-testing.pdf). This version has every button working and progress pages between tasks.
- **Design document:** [`docs/fixopolis-design-document.pdf`](docs/fixopolis-design-document.pdf)

Open the PDFs in Adobe Acrobat or the Chrome/Edge PDF viewer so the links work.

## Test tasks

1. You have noticed a pothole on the road outside your home. Report it to the council.
2. A few weeks ago you reported a broken footpath on Hill St. Find out what is happening with it.
3. A neighbour has already reported a broken streetlight on Wattle St. It affects you too. Show your support for their report.

## Usability test mode (interactive prototype)

On the start screen, switch on **Test mode**. The prototype runs the three tasks in order, records time, taps, wrong taps and back presses, then shows the SUS questionnaire. In Chrome or Edge you choose a results folder once, and `results_tasks.csv` and `results_survey.csv` fill in automatically with an incrementing participant ID.
